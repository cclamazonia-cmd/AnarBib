### Why CC-BY-SA-4.0 for documentation?

The AnarBib **codebase** is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)** — a strong copyleft license appropriate for software, particularly server-side software intended to remain free across networked deployments.

For **documentation, policies, and legal templates**, however, software licenses are a poor fit: their terminology («source code», «executable», «linking») does not map cleanly onto written texts. The Creative Commons CC-BY-SA-4.0 license is designed for textual and creative works, and its ShareAlike clause carries the same political intent as the AGPL: any adapted version must remain freely available to others under equivalent terms.

Adherent libraries are explicitly **encouraged** to:

1. **Copy** these documents into their own repositories and infrastructure.
2. **Adapt** them to reflect their local jurisdiction, language, governance structure, and political context.
3. **Republish** the adapted versions, ideally back-contributing improvements to the AnarBib project.

This includes the **GDPR record of processing activities** (`REGISTRE_TRAITEMENTS.md`) and the **incident response procedure** (`INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md`), which are intentionally written as both templates and worked examples.

### Translations

Translations of these documents into other languages are considered **adaptations** under the CC-BY-SA-4.0 license. They must:

- Credit the original AnarBib documentation.
- Be released under CC-BY-SA-4.0 (or a compatible license).
- Indicate clearly that they are translations and identify the source version.

We particularly welcome translations into Portuguese (Brazilian), Spanish (with Argentine militant inclusive conventions), Italian (avoiding fascist-tradition terminology — see the AnarBib inclusive language charter), German (using Genderstern conventions), and other languages of the militant library network.
